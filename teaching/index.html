Fun Workshop

I founded and organize Fun Workshop, a series of cross-disciplinary talks and hands-on sessions at XJTLU. The aim is to offer interesting intellectual experiences and candid insights, with a welcome for people of every background and skill level.

The series brings together support from university departments, schools, student societies, and external organizations, with at least seven sessions per semester.

ClawTwin Initiative

In summer 2026, I brought together more than 20 enthusiasts from different backgrounds to explore how agents belonging to different people could interact. My work on Agent Comm grew alongside this effort, connecting technical experiments with questions about human control and collaboration.

Supporting the postgraduate community

I have edited the XJTLU Postgraduate Unofficial Guide since 2024, helping make the experience of postgraduate study easier to navigate.

  • Vice President, XJTLU Postgraduate Research Society, 2023–2024.
  • Postgraduate representative on the XJTLU Research Committee and Taicang campus committee, 2023–2024.
  • Chair, School of Internet of Things postgraduate student communication committee, preparing reports on students’ needs.
  • Session chair and organizer at the XJTLU Postgraduate Research Symposium, 2025 and 2026.

Teaching & communication

As a teaching assistant, I deliver approximately 20 hours of undergraduate teaching per semester. I enjoy explaining complex ideas through concrete examples and discussion, and have received several best oral presentation awards at postgraduate research forums.
